What We Do

At Amazing Charity Group, we turn compassion into action. Our work focuses on meeting urgent needs while building long-term solutions that transform lives and strengthen communities.

We achieve this by:

Providing Essential Support ⁃ Supplying food, clothing, and basic necessities to individuals and families in need.

Empowering Through Education ⁃ Offering scholarships, learning resources, and mentorship to give children and youth a brighter future.

Improving Health & Wellbeing ⁃ Supporting access to healthcare, awareness programs, and wellness initiatives for vulnerable groups.

Community Development ⁃ Creating sustainable projects that promote self-reliance, skill-building, and economic opportunity.

Advocacy & Awareness ⁃ Raising voices for the marginalized and promoting positive change through community engagement and partnerships.

Every program we run is guided by a single purpose: to bring hope, dignity, and opportunity to those who need it most.

About Us

At Amazing Charity Group, we believe that every act of kindness has the power to change lives. Our mission is to bring hope, support, and opportunity to those in need by working hand in hand with communities, partners, and volunteers.

Founded on the values of compassion, integrity, and inclusivity, we are dedicated to creating lasting impact through initiatives that address real challenges—whether it's fighting hunger, supporting education, providing healthcare, or empowering families to build brighter futures.

We are more than just a charity-we are a community of change-makers united by a shared vision: a world where no one is left behind. Every donation, every volunteer hour, and every partnership fuels our ability to reach those who need it most.

Together, we can turn empathy into action and hope into reality.

Join us in making a difference.

AMAZING CHARITY GROUP.

P.O. Box 30-60103, Runyenjes.

The group is established as a non-political, non-sectarian, non-tribal voluntary charity group of members brought together by the urge and passion of helping the needy, less priveleged persons in the society.
The members of the group come fron different walks of life. The group comprises of persons diverse in culture, age, religion and briefs but bound together by love and compassion with the goal of uplifting the lives of vulnerable members of the community through compassion, empowerment and sustainable support programs.

PURPOSE OF THE GROUP

  • Supporting individuals and families facing financial hardship.
  • Providing essential resources like food, shelter, and healthcare to vulnerable populations.
  • Offering assistance to those affected by natural disasters or other emergencies.
  • Promoting educational opportunities for children and adults.
  • Supporting literacy programs and initiatives.
  • Providing access to learning resources and educational materials.
  • Fostering social cohesion and community involvement.
  • Supporting local organizations and initiatives that address community needs.

SOURCES OF FUNDS

To facilitate goals of the group, the members seek to raise funds throught:
– Members registration fee and monthly subscriptions.
– Donations.
– Fundraising activities.
– Investments.
– Grants and Sponsorships from government agencies, NGOs and/or partners.
– Any other source approved by the members.

ELIGIBILITY FOR MEMBERSHIP

a) Must believe in and support the group’s mission of charity, compassion and community development
b) Should be honest, responsible and respected in the community
c) Should be ready to participate actively in meetings, projects and community service.
d) Must agree to follow the group’s bylaws, leadership and decisions made democratically.
e) Should complete the membership process by paying Kshs. 1000/= non-refundable.
f) Individual with past unethical behavior or misuse of the funds maybe disqualified to protect the group’s integrity.
g) Should be 18 years and above.
